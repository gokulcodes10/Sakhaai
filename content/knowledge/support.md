# How support works

Sakha, the assistant on this site, is the front door to Asphalix support. It
answers from our documentation first and hands you to a person whenever it
cannot help, or whenever you ask.

## Getting help

- **Signed-in clients** can ask Sakha to raise a ticket, check on an existing
  one, or add detail to it. Tickets also live in the client portal under
  Support tickets, where you can reply and close them.
- **Anyone else** can ask Sakha to talk to a human. Sakha asks for your name and
  email, attaches the conversation, and a person replies by email.

## Response times

- A person reads every new ticket and replies within 24 hours.
- **Urgent** means a production agent is down or acting wrongly in front of
  your customers. Urgent tickets are picked up as soon as they are seen.

## What to include in a ticket

- What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.
- When it started, and whether it is still happening.
- Which agent or workflow is involved, and an example (a message, an order
  number, a screenshot description).
- Whether customers are affected right now.

## Ticket statuses

- **Open** — with the Asphalix team.
- **Waiting on you** — we replied and need something from you. Replying moves it
  back to open.
- **Resolved** — we think it is fixed. Reply if it is not and it reopens.
- **Closed** — done. If the problem comes back, raise a new ticket and mention
  the old number.

## What support does not cover

Contract, commercial and invoice questions go to a founder, not the support
queue. Sakha will route those to a person directly.
