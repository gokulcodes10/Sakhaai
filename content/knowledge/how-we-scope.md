# How we scope an engagement

Asphalix does not publish prices. Every engagement is scoped in a written build
memo after a 30-minute call with a founder, so nobody has to guess and nobody
gets a number that was not built for their situation.

## The two things that actually drive scope

**How many systems we have to touch.** One clean system with an API is a small
job. Four systems, one of which is a spreadsheet a person maintains by hand, is
a bigger one. Every integration is a place where things can differ from the
documentation.

**The state of the data inside them.** Duplicate customer records, three
spellings of the same product, order statuses that mean different things in
different departments. This is where most AI projects quietly fail, and it is
why we do the data audit in week one rather than at the end.

## What does not change the scope

- How large your company is.
- How urgent it is. We cannot buy time, and pretending otherwise produces a
  worse system.
- Whether you are the first client in your industry.

## When you get the number

In the written build memo, after the first call, before you commit to anything.
If the data audit in week one changes our view, we tell you immediately rather
than at the invoice.

## Payment

Staged against delivery, not against the calendar: the prototype, then the
working system, then the handover.
