Each one is now a real mark on the v2 palette and caps wordmark, taken far enough to judge: emblem, horizontal lockup, favicon at true size, app tile, and both mono cuts. Each also carries the problem it still has. Reference them as 2a, 2b, 2c, 2d.

Three courses laid short-to-long: a base being built up, and the only direction that makes the 4-hue palette feel structural rather than decorative. Strongest at 16px of the four.

Risk: horizontal bars are the universal menu glyph. Offsetting them helps; in a browser toolbar it will still read as a control at first glance.

Two open frames that overprint where they meet — the shared zone is literally drawn, which is a good picture of two systems trusting each other. Held to two links, not four, to stay minimal.

MY READ

2a is the only one of the four I would put against the arch: it holds at 16px, it uses the palette structurally, and "courses laid down" is the same infrastructure story. 2c is the safest and the most anonymous. 2b and 2d both have a structural problem — one needs a light ground, the other cannot hold four hues.
