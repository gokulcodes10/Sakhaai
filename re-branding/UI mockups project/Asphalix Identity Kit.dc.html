The mark

why it looks like this
SUBSTRATE

Asphalt is the layer everything else runs on and nobody thinks about. That is the promise of self-hosted infrastructure — so the mark is built like roadwork, not like a spark or a brain.

CONVERGENCE

Two lanes rising to a single apex: many agents, one orchestrated outcome. It also draws a clean capital A without spelling it out cheaply.

KEYSTONE

The amber keystone sits where load transfers — the model you own, inside your own perimeter. It is the only element allowed to carry colour.

Construction & minimums

x = keystone width

Clear space on all four sides equals one keystone width (x). Nothing — type, rules, photo edges — enters that field. In the horizontal lockup the wordmark sits 0.7x from the mark and aligns to its optical centre.
