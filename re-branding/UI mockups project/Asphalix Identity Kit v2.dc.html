01

The idea

safety · security · certainty
ROOT

Greek asphalēs — not liable to fall. Literally un-slipping. That is the whole brief: certainty, delivered by things that do not move when loaded.

FORM

An arch holds because of its keystone: the harder you press, the tighter it locks. Two piers, one keystone, one protected span. No shield, no padlock, no brain.

WHY THREE PARTS

Three members means three colours without decoration — the palette is structural, so a four-product suite already has somewhere to live.

04

Clear space & minimums

x = keystone width

The emblem sits on a 64-unit grid: piers 13 wide rotated 10°, keystone 18 × 13. Clear space equals one keystone width (x = 18u) on all four sides. In the horizontal lockup the wordmark starts 0.8x from the emblem and centres on the arch span.

Emblem alone: 16px minimum on screen, 6mm in print. Below 20px, drop to a two-colour build (both piers in one hue) so the shapes stay separable. The full horizontal lockup never goes below 120px wide or 25mm.
